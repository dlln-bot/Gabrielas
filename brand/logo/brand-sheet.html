The mark

One ribbon, one twist, one G.

The mark is a single ribbon-gymnastics ribbon, released from the stick and caught mid-turn. As it sweeps round it twists once: the ink face turns away and its rouge lining shows. The crossbar of the G is the stick, and the rouge dot is the dancer's hand holding it.

It is drawn the same way as the dance lines on gabrielas.se, from a parametric curve rather than by hand, so the logo and the website's motion come from the same family.

Use

A few rules

  • Clear space on every side: the height of the G's crossbar gap (about ¼ of the mark's height).
  • Minimum size: horizontal lockup 140 px wide, mark 24 px, favicon cut below 48 px.
  • Rouge stays on the ribbon's lining and the hand. Don't recolour the front face.
  • On photographs, use the paper or ink mono version over a calm area of the image.
  • Don't rotate, outline, add shadows, or redraw the ribbon by hand. Regenerate it from the script.
  • The live, breathing version is for screens only: site intro, reels, the lobby display.
